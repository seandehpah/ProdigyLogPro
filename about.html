About Us

Prodigy Logistics & Procurement Services is the specialized arm of The Prodigy Consults Limited, dedicated to providing reliable, end-to-end logistics, supply chain coordination, and procurement solutions to businesses across Nigeria.

Rooted in extensive real-world operations, our mission is to simplify sourcing, distribution, and overall supply chain management. We partner with manufacturers, packaging firms, retailers, and institutional buyers to tackle their most complex challenges, from raw material and equipment sourcing to critical last-mile delivery.

Our tailored solutions ensure speed, precision, and professionalism in every aspect of your logistics and procurement needs.

Operational bases: Lagos (HQ), Abuja, Port Harcourt, Plateau State.
